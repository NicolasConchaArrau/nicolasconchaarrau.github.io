Bear, base and bull scenarios

Initial capital only, Excel dashboard method: capital × (1 + μ ± 1.65σ)^years.

The template assumes a 5th or 95th percentile year repeats every year, so its bear and bull cases are extreme bounds. The standard cone lets good and bad years offset (risk grows with √t), so it gives the realistic 90% range for the horizon.

What this tab includes

This tab goes beyond the Excel model of the course. Read these limits before using its figures.

Included in this tab
  • Chile taxes as a yearly drag on returns: none, APV Régimen A, Art. 107 (10%) or Global Complementario at the chosen bracket.
  • The same tax treatment for the current and the target portfolio, so the comparison stays fair.
  • Taxes in retirement: they lower the withdrawal the capital can sustain, so the capital needed rises.
  • Inflation: all figures are in today's money and contributions rise with inflation.
  • Uncertainty: 5,000 simulated futures; the required saving is shown at 50%, 75% and 90% confidence.
Not included
  • Taxes in the other tabs: Overview, Efficient frontier, Allocation, Transition plan and Risk are pre-tax, like the Excel model they are validated against.
  • The APV state bonus (15%) and the yearly APV limits.
  • The Art. 57 exemption for mutual fund redemptions (up to 30 UTM a year).
  • Tax on currency gains from investing in US dollars, and USD/CLP exchange-rate risk.
  • Fund and broker fees, which lower returns in the same way as taxes.
  • Taxes of another country of residence (for example Spain).
  • A rule to live only off returns: set the withdrawal rate to about 4.2% (half the futures keep the capital intact) or 3% (about three in four).
Not tax or financial advice. Tax rules change and depend on residence; check with a tax adviser before acting.

Validation against Excel model v7

Recomputed with the model default inputs (medium-high profile, 20% VaR limit), whatever is entered on the left.

Scope. These checks cover the pre-tax model replicated from the Excel, including the v7 additions (probability cone, unconstrained frontier and monthly tail). The parametric monthly VaR is checked to 0.05 pp because the Excel reports it rounded. The Retirement goal tab adds taxes, inflation and simulations that are not in the Excel model, so it is checked separately (against an independent Python simulation), not here.
Method. Portfolio return = Σ wᵢμᵢ. Volatility = √(wᵀΣw), with Σ built from the asset volatilities and the correlation matrix. Sharpe = (return − risk-free) / volatility. The projection uses the same closed form as the Client Outcome dashboard. The transition path simulates each asset class month by month and sends each contribution to the classes furthest below target.
